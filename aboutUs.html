Our origin

From a concert to a bottle

Last year, Max went to a concert at the Stanford Ampitheater, and brought shooters to enjoy a buzz while listening to the music. After struggling to take shots of 99-proof alcohol or a malt-cinnamon whiskey, he wished he had the tequila and lime combo from his rooftop pregame in San Francisco.

He approached Sam with the idea: a shot-and-chaser which recreates the pregame on-the-go, while still giving you the buzz he was looking for.

2023: Inspiration strikes at a concert. The Kickback's team is created.
2024: R&D lead to our first bottle and our first patent.
2025: Mass production begins. First ever chaser formula is created.
2026: Statewide rollout with help from the Kickback's community.
